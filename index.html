Ontario G1 High-Yield Numbers Cheat Sheet

Rapid pre-exam review of the exact figures tested by the MTO

Licensing & Admin
  • 16 years: Minimum age for G1
  • 80%: Pass mark on BOTH Signs & Rules
  • 4 years: Class G experience for G1 mentor
  • 12 mos (8 w/ BDE): G1 wait; 12 mos G2 wait
  • 12 a.m. – 5 a.m.: G1 driving curfew
  • 6 days: Report name/address change to MTO
  • 60 days: Exchange out-of-province licence
  • 80+ years: Renew every 2 years
Speeds, Lights & Distances
  • 50 km/h: Unposted city/town/village
  • 80 km/h: Unposted rural road
  • 100 km/h: 400-series freeway (110 if posted)
  • 2 seconds: Minimum dry following distance
  • 30 min: Headlights before sunset / after sunrise
  • 150 m: Dim high beams (oncoming) / visibility rule
  • 60 m: Dim high beams (following)
  • 150 m: Stay back from responding fire truck
Parking, Buses & Sharing
  • 3 m: Fire hydrant no-parking zone
  • 9 m / 15 m: Corner / traffic-light intersection
  • 15 m: No parking from railway crossing
  • 5 m: Stop from nearest rail for a train
  • 20 m: Stop behind flashing school bus
  • 2 m: Stop behind open streetcar doors
  • 1 m: Minimum clearance passing cyclists
  • 40 km/h or less: Slow-moving vehicle triangle
Points, Impairment & Crashes
  • 0.00% BAC: G1/G2 & all drivers age 21 & under
  • 0.05% / 0.08%: Warn range / Criminal limit
  • 2 years: Demerit points stay from offence date
  • 9 pts / 15 pts: Suspension for Novice (60d) / Full G (30d)
  • 40+ / 50+ over: Stunt driving (<80 / ≥80 km/h)
  • $5,000: Mandatory police crash report threshold
  • 1.5 mm: Minimum legal tire tread depth
  • 8 yrs / 36 kg / 145 cm: Booster seat graduation
